🎯 Core Objectives
￼ ♻️ Repurpose Waste: Utilize cardboard, plastics, and wooden skewers to construct a sturdy mechanical chassis.
￼ ⚙️ Mechanical Drive: Implement simple machines (rotational axles, rubber-band motors, and propulsion fans).
￼ 📊 Quantify Performance: Calculate surface area, wheel circumferences, friction coefficients, and mechanical advantages.
￼ 💻 Open-Source Documentation: Structure and publish full technical plans using GitHub and Markdown standards.

🧪 1. Science (Physics & Ecology)
￼ Energy Transformation: Stores Potential Elastic Energy (￼) in wound-up rubber bands and converts it into Kinetic Energy (￼) when released.
￼ Aerodynamics & Propulsion: Air-propeller blades push air backward, generating an equal and opposite forward thrust force (Newton's 3rd Law of Motion).
￼ Friction Control: Straws are used as smooth axle bushings to reduce rotational friction.
📐 2. Mathematics (Geometry & Measurements)
￼ Chassis Volume & Area: Geometric surface area analysis for lightweight structural stability.
￼ Kinematics: Calculating velocity (￼) and wheel revolution counts based on circumference.
💻 3. Information Technology (IT & Open Source)
￼ Digital Version Control: Managing project files, schematics, and updates on GitHub.
￼ Markdown Formatting: Utilizing HTML tags, badges, LaTeX code, and tables inside a single web-rendered ⁠README.md⁠.
📝 4. English (Technical Communication)
￼ Technical Writing: Formulating clear, step-by-step assembly guides and safety warnings.
￼ Public Speaking & Defense: Presenting project outcomes and reflecting on environmental impact during the final assessment.
